The operation

Name the hands that beat you. Count only those. Compare against what he would take the same line with that you beat.

The four numbers

Pocket pair6
Suited4
Offsuit12
Both16

Every card you can see removes combos. A pair with one card on the board drops from 6 to 3. An offsuit hand with one card gone drops from 12 to 9.

Why the small side is the one you count

The hands that beat you are countable, usually under 20. His missed hands run in the dozens and you only need to know they are bigger. Never try to count the big side.

Bets and raises are different

A bet can be a lot of missed hands, so one pair is usually a call. A raise is narrow and almost always made of hands that got there, so the same pair is usually a fold. The count is what tells them apart.

What this assumes

He bets top pair or better for value and raises two pair or better. His bluff rate is the read shown on each hand. Equity is computed exactly, by enumerating every river card on turn spots.
