Halve the effective stack and you halve the SPR. A 50bb villain in a 3-bet pot puts you at SPR 2, not 4.4, and top pair becomes a stack-off.

Pick the size backward from the river. At SPR 6 with a set, start at 2/3 pot and the river shove arrives on its own. Start at 1/3 and you leave 40bb behind.

The routine at the table

1Read the effective stack in bb before you act preflop. Smaller of the two.
2Count the pot when the flop comes.
3Divide.
4Pick the flop size that makes the river bet natural.

The spot that costs money on Ignition is the 40bb reg. In a 3-bet pot that is SPR 1.5, and AK on an ace-high flop is a stack-off rather than a two-street value hand. Check the stack before the hand starts.
